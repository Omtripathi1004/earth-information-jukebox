What you are hearing

WaterLow, soft sine pad with a long tail
VegetationWarm triangle tone
Bare land, citiesBright, sharper plucked tone
Cloud, ice, snowHigh bell
BrightnessPitch, on a two-octave pentatonic scale (any note sounds consonant)
Texture / contrastLoudness: busy coastlines and city edges play louder than flat desert or ocean
Scan line positionStereo pan, left to right
AltitudeLow drone that brightens as you fly closer to the ground
NASA data framesColour becomes pitch: blue is low, red is high. Grey means no data and is silent
Local climateEach year of NASA POWER temperature (1985–2025) becomes one note

Shortcuts: L start or stop listening · scroll, the + and − buttons or the zoom slider to move between orbit and ground.

About and data credits

An independent prototype made for the NASA Space Apps Challenge. It is not an official NASA product, and NASA does not endorse it.

  • NASA EOSDIS GIBS: satellite imagery and data layers. NASA POWER: local climate.
  • Esri World Imagery, AWS Terrain Tiles, OpenStreetMap and OpenFreeMap: base imagery, terrain, buildings and labels.
  • Street photos: © Mapillary contributors, CC BY-SA 4.0.
  • Open-source software: MapLibre GL JS, Tailwind CSS, CesiumJS, Flask, NumPy, Pillow and Requests. Esri imagery and Google photoreal 3D are third-party services with their own terms. Full list and licences: SOURCES.md in the repository.
  • Photoreal 3D: Google Photorealistic 3D Tiles through Cesium ion, used under their terms.
